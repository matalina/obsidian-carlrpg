# Race and class build points — benefits and detriments

## Where each one goes on a race or class note

| Benefit or detriment | Property | Worked out by the plugin |
| --- | --- | --- |
| +1 to a Stat, Stat detriments | `bonuses: { str: 1 }` (negative for detriments) | Yes — unenhanced stat |
| Points split between stats | `stat-points: { points: 3, stats: [str, dex] }`, crawler sets `race-stats` / `class-stats` | Yes — unenhanced stat |
| +1 in a Skill or Spell, Skill detriments | `skills:` / `spells:` | Yes — skill rank |
| Natural attack (1d6 or 1d8) | `skills: { "[[Bite]]": F }` plus an attack skill note for it | Yes — rank = floor, in Skills and Attacks |
| +1 DR Buff | `bonuses: { dr: 1 }` | Yes — DR |
| +5ft Move, +5 Step | `bonuses: { move: 5, step: 5 }` | Yes — Move / Step |
| Size 2 (Small) or less | `size:` | Yes — size |
| Everything else | `abilities:` as text | No — shown on the card and the sheet |


## Minor benefits (1 build point each)

- +1 to a Stat
- Membership in Club Desperado or Club Vanquisher
- At the end of each floor, add 1 to your Skill Advancement Checks for a particular Skill
- Allow a specific Skill to be raised to Rank 20
- Add your Stat Mod a second time when using a specific non-combat Skill in a common circumstance
- A benefit that has a condition, such as gaining Advantage when attacking stone creatures
- Can see in total darkness or has a natural ability to produce light
- Double Mana regeneration from mending and resting in a specific environment
- You can see twice as far as most creatures
- A tier-1 crafting table (as soon as you acquire a personal space, it shows up there)
- A free room in saferooms
- Discount at stores, bonus earned from sales or interest earned (+1 for each category)
- Gain a non-combat benefit once per session under very specific conditions

## Moderate benefits (2 build points each)

- +1 in a Skill or Spell, including Passive ones (you cannot have more than 5 points of Passive Skills in any Class build)
- Ability to heal 1 Health Bar slot when you damage or kill a creature with a specific Weapon Skill or Spell, up to 5 Health Bar per combat scene
- Add your Stat Mod a second time when making an attack in a common circumstance
- Roll with Advantage when making Skill Advancement Checks for a particular Skill
- At the end of each floor, add 1 to your Skill Advancement Checks for a particular group of thematically linked Skills
- At the start of combat, you can change your damage to a different type for that combat
- +1 DR Buff (to a limit of +3)
- Can access any guild of a particular type (for example, any Weapon Training Guild)
- Double Mana regeneration from mending and resting without a limit
- Membership in all professional and social clubs
- Membership in the Dungeon Book of the Floor Club
- Can raise a thematically linked group of Skills to Rank 20
- Mobs drop 1 × Floor Number gold when you kill them with a specific attack
- Ability to gain Popularity with specific actions and attacks
- Patron Benefit
- Resistance to an uncommon damage type (Force, Psychic, Necrotic, etc.)
- Resistance to damage dealt by the environment (Falling, Drowning, dungeon traps, etc.)
- Replace a Stat used in an ability with another Stat (normally, one that receives a Stat bonus), such as the ability to apply Charisma to Intimidate instead of Strength
- A Stat Mod from another ability be used in addition to the normal Mod for specific Skills
- Can use skills designed for one type of weapon on another type of weapon
- When you use a Healing-type Spell, the target heals 1 additional Health Bar
- The ability to traverse certain terrain types without Skill Checks
- The ability to burrow
- The ability to breathe underwater
- +1 in all Skills for a temporary duration that's an hour or less
- A natural attack at [Rank = Floor Number] that deals 1d6 + Stat Mod damage. Choose the Stat Mod to hit and damage (and damage type). Add another d6 at Rank 5, 10, and 15. Rank only increases by Floor
- The ability to reassign a Race or Class's Stat point benefits once per floor during a Long Rest

## Major benefits (3 build points each)

- Access to significant assistance, akin to the Manager benefit
- Gain a friendly Pet or a Mount
- Ability to provide a significant Buff or Benefit up to 4 Points to all of your Pets or Mounts
- An ability to dramatically alter an encounter once a day, such as by making enemies attack each other
- A defensive ability usable once per day, such as negating all damage you take from a single attack
- The ability to Buff the entire party once per day, such as granting them regeneration or extra DR
- The ability to fly, with some limitations
- The ability to survive without breathing, which also gives Immunity to inhaled toxins
- Rage (Benefit): Your melee attacks deal +1 damage for each Health Bar slot you have lost
- Size 2 (Small) or less
- +5ft Move (this increases the crawler's Move value)
- +5 Step (this increases the crawler's Step value)
- Add 1d4 to your Evade or Stat Checks for a specific Stat
- A natural attack at [Rank = Floor Number] that deals 1d8 + Stat Mod damage. Choose the Stat Mod to hit and damage (and damage type). Add another d8 at Rank 5, 10, and 15. Rank only increases by Floor
- Advantage when using a specific non-combat Skill or Spell
- Situational bonus of up to ×2 damage for one round
- Swap Con for determining Health Bar values with another Stat
- Gain a Climb Movement, which allows you to automatically climb most surfaces, even ceilings

## Extreme benefits (4 build points each)

- +1 in a Skill that you wouldn't normally be able to learn, such as Cockroach and Light on Your Feet
- +1 to all Skills in a thematically linked group of 3–5 Skills (Hand-to-Hand Skills, Constitution-based Skills, etc.)
- Advantage on all Stat Checks for a specific Stat
- All Skills in a thematically linked group of up to 6 Skills can be raised to Rank 20
- Immunity to Poison
- Resistance to a common damage type (Bludgeoning, Fire)
- Double the duration of your Rank 5 and lower Spells that have a duration
- Advantage on all Skills in a limited non-combat situation
- Additional functional arm
- Doppelgänger shape-changing

## Epic benefits (6 build points each)

- +1 to all Skills in a thematically linked group of Skills (Ranged Weapons, Intelligence-based Skills, etc.)
- All Skills can be raised to Rank 20
- A significant defensive ability, such as Immunity to a common damage type (like Bludgeoning, Fire or Piercing) or halving the damage of the first nine attacks against you each day
- The Limb Regeneration Benefit
- Roll with Advantage on all Skills in a wide-ranging set of circumstances
- Immunity to Poison and all diseases
- +1 in all Skills in a thematically linked group of 6+ Skills (Ranged Weapons, Intelligence-based Skills, etc.)
- A significant defensive ability, such as Advantage on Evade Checks, or Immunity to a common type of damage (Fire, Bludgeoning, etc.)
- A powerful and flexible ability that can significantly change your Stats or Skills, such as a Changeling's ability to assume another Race's Stat or Skill bonuses
- Changeling shapeshifting
- Roll with Advantage on all Skill Checks for a specific Stat
- The ability to fly with no limitations

## Minor detriments (1 extra build point)

- Cannot choose Class due to membership in a specific club
- For every −2 worth of Stat detriments
- For every −2 worth of Skill detriments
- For every −1 to all Skills under a specific Stat
- +1 Mana cost to cast spells not favored by your Class
- Disadvantage when using a thematically linked group of Skills, when there is a condition (such as Disadvantage in all social Skills used against a certain category of creatures)
- Must worship a deity
- Cannot worship a deity
- You suffer a −1 penalty when making Skill Advancement Checks for a specific Skill provided by your Race or Class at Rank 5+
- Vulnerability: An uncommon damage type (such as Force or Necrotic) deals double damage to you
- You halve the effects of a broad type of damage, such as all Weapon damage or all Spell damage, that isn't the focus of your Class (such as halving Spell damage if you are a Barbarian)
- A weakness that has two conditions, such as Disadvantage in social situations when dealing with Fairies
- Delay a bonus to after Level 50 or activating on the 6th Floor

## Moderate detriments (2 extra build points)

- A weakness with a single broad condition, such as Disadvantage in all social situations
- Skill only increases in Rank upon descent to the next floor
- Limited choice of weapons available to choose from
- No Stat Mod added to Weapon Skill provided by your Race or Class
- +3 Mana cost to a Class of spells provided by your Race or Class
- Disadvantage when using a thematically linked group of Skills, such as all movement-related Skills
- Prohibited from using a wide category of useful items, such as an inability to use certain types of Weapons or to cast Passive Spells

## Major detriments (3 extra build points)

- A specific Stat is capped at 10. Even magic gear and potions cannot exceed this limit
- Vulnerability: A common damage type (such as Bludgeoning or Piercing) deals double damage to you
